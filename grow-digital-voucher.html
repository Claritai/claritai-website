Fund new business software with the Grow Digital Voucher

Eligible small businesses can get 50% of qualifying costs — from a minimum of €500 up to €5,000 — toward new off-the-shelf software and related setup or training.

Check eligibility (30 seconds) →

Eligibility and approval are determined solely by your Local Enterprise Office.

Grow Digital Voucher — quick check

Answer 5 quick questions to see if your business is likely to qualify.

1. Is your business trading for 6 months or more?

2. Does the business have between 1 and 50 paid employees?

3. Have you completed a Digital for Business project (with your LEO) in the last two years?

4. Does the business have current Revenue tax clearance?

5. Is the business currently outside Enterprise Ireland and IDA Ireland support?

What it can fund

New off-the-shelf business software (e-commerce, website, CRM, booking, stock control, accounting, cybersecurity, analytics), plus related IT configuration and training capped at 50% of the project.

What it cannot fund

Custom or bespoke software, compliance-only systems, routine upgrades, and any costs incurred before your application is approved.
