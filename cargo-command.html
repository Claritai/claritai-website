The hub

One workspace built for how logistics teams actually work.

Logistics runs on a hundred small things happening at once — emails landing in a shared mailbox, a CVRT falling due, a trailer that needs locating, a driving ban on a Friday, a quote that hinges on fuel cost. Most operators juggle all of it across Outlook, spreadsheets, portals and sticky notes.

Cargo Command pulls those threads together. It is a single, secure operations hub that sits on top of your existing Microsoft 365 and gives your whole team one shared view of the working day — fully branded as your own.

  • One live view of inboxes, calendar, fleet and trailers
  • Every key compliance date tracked and surfaced before it falls due
  • Powered by your own Microsoft 365 — not another data silo
  • An AI Ops Agent that reads the whole hub and drafts what is next

From scattered tabs to a single source of truth.

The cost of a fragmented operation is rarely one big failure — it is the steady drip of missed dates, duplicated effort and information that lives in one person's head.

A typical day without Cargo Command

  • Shared mailboxes checked in Outlook, with no team-wide view of what is outstanding.
  • Compliance dates — CVRT, tax, insurance, tacho, CPC — in a spreadsheet nobody updates.
  • Trailer locations chased across three different tracking portals and bookmarks.
  • Fuel quotes worked out by hand or guessed from memory.
  • Road closures and driving bans found out about after a truck is already rolling.
  • Every new starter needs to be shown where everything lives.

The same day on Cargo Command

  • Every shared inbox on one dashboard, with live counts and a team-wide view.
  • Expiries surfaced automatically — the ones due soonest pushed to the top.
  • All tracking portals one click away from a single Trailer Tracking screen.
  • Fuel cost for any route estimated in seconds, per truck.
  • Logistics news, road closures and country driving bans in one live feed.
  • New staff sign in with Microsoft and see the same hub as everyone else.

What each module actually does.

Seven modules that each work on their own and share one branded, secure workspace — with the Ops Agent watching across all of them.

📨
Shared Inbox Hub

Every shared mailbox on one dashboard

Cargo Command reads your Microsoft 365 shared mailboxes live and presents them as one operations dashboard — inbox counts, sent and deleted folders, hypercare, top clients and reporting side by side. Admins add or remove which mailboxes appear from a self-service screen, with no code changes.

  • Team-wide visibility — everyone sees the same inboxes and outstanding work, not just whoever owns the mailbox.
  • Self-service inbox manager — an admin adds a new mailbox in seconds; grant access in 365 and it appears.
  • No new logins — mail is read through each user's own Microsoft sign-in, so access follows your existing 365 permissions.
📅
Operations Calendar

Your Outlook calendar, built into the hub

Schedules, meetings and key dates sync straight from Outlook into Cargo Command. View the whole month or drop into a focused week view, and create, edit or colour-code events without leaving the workspace.

  • One place for the plan — the operational calendar lives beside the inboxes and fleet, not in a separate app.
  • Month & week views — zoom out for the shape of the week, in for the detail of the day.
  • Two-way — events created in Cargo Command write back to your real Outlook calendar.
🚛
Fleet & Compliance — expiry tracker

Vehicles, drivers and every key date in one place

A single register of your vehicles and drivers with the dates that keep you legal — and it does the watching for you. Expiries falling due soonest are surfaced automatically so nothing slips.

  • Per vehicle — registration, CVRT, motor tax, insurance, tacho calibration, next service and 13-week safety check.
  • Per driver — licence expiry, driver CPC and tachograph card expiry.
  • Nothing expires unnoticed — upcoming dates are pushed to the top and flagged on the home screen's attention strip.
📍
Trailer Tracking

Every tracking portal, one click away

Track trailers and loads in real time across the fleet. Cargo Command gives you a single Trailer Tracking screen with direct links to whichever tracking portals you use, so the team stops hunting through bookmarks to locate a unit.

  • One launchpad — all your telematics and trailer portals from one screen.
  • Faster answers — "where's that trailer?" is a single click, not a search for the right login page.
  • Yours to configure — add, rename or remove portal links yourself, no developer needed.
⛽
Fuel Calculator

Cost any route in seconds

Work out fuel use and cost for a route, per truck. Enter the journey and Cargo Command estimates distance on a live route map, applies the truck's consumption and your fuel price, and returns the fuel needed and the cost — ready to feed a quote.

  • Quote with confidence — price jobs on real numbers instead of gut feel.
  • Per-truck accuracy — each vehicle's consumption is factored in, not a fleet average.
  • Live route map — see the route as you cost it, powered by Google mapping.
📰
Logistics News

Road status and driving bans, before the wheels turn

A live feed of Irish, UK and EU logistics news, organised into tabs for latest news, road closures and driving bans — including country-by-country HGV bans and links to official traffic sources. The team sees disruption early, not after a truck is already committed to a blocked route.

  • Fewer nasty surprises — weekend and holiday lorry bans and closures surfaced in advance.
  • Sorted by what matters — closures and bans live on their own tabs, not buried in a news stream.
  • Curated sources — feeds are configurable per customer, so the news stays relevant to your lanes.
🎨
White-label branding

Make it unmistakably yours

Cargo Command is multi-tenant and white-label. From an in-app admin screen you set the product name, tagline, logo and colour scheme — applied instantly across the login, home screen, sidebar and notifications. Your staff and customers see your brand; Claritai powers it behind the scenes.

  • Your brand, front and centre — a hub that looks like your company, not a generic third-party tool.
  • Change it yourself, any time — no rebuild, no ticket, no wait.
  • One product, many tenants — every customer gets their own branded, isolated instance.
